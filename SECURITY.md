# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not report security issues through public GitHub issues, discussions or pull requests.

Report them privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/sarvalabs/js-mal-sdk/security/advisories/new). Include:

- the affected component (`contracts/` or the SDK) and version or commit
- a description of the issue and its impact
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept

We will acknowledge your report, keep you updated while we investigate, and credit you in the advisory once a fix is released, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

## Scope

- The AuthorityRegistry and MoiIdentity logics in `contracts/`
- The JavaScript/TypeScript SDK
